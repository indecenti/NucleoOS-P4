# Pomodoro Desk Hub

## What it's for

A desk dashboard for working in blocks with the Pomodoro method: 25 minutes of focus, then a break. On the same screen you get a clock, the month's calendar and how many pomodoros you've done against your goal.

## How to use it

1. Tap the **TASK** row to choose what you're working on (six fixed tasks, one more per tap).
2. Pick the block: **FOCUS (25M)**, **BREAK (5M)** or **LONG (15M)**.
3. Tap **START**. The ring of ticks around the time fills up as it runs.
4. When a focus block ends, the pomodoro is added to your stats and the app switches to the 5-minute break; when a break ends it goes back to focus. Start the next block with **START**.

## Controls

- **START / PAUSE**: starts or pauses the time.
- **RESET**: puts the current block back to its full length.
- **-1 MIN, +1 MIN, +5 MIN**: shorten or lengthen the running block.
- **< and >** on the calendar: previous or next month.
- **- and +** next to DAILY GOAL: how many pomodoros you want to do (2 to 12).
- **CLEAR**: erases the pomodoros done and the total focus time.
- **NIGHT**: black screen with the time and clock in red and low brightness, for the bedside table. Tap the screen to return to the dashboard.
- **AUDIO**: each tap cycles VOICE, BEEP and OFF.
- **UTC**: see below.
- To exit, use the Back gesture (swipe right from the left edge): the app asks for confirmation, tap **EXIT**. Swiping up from the bottom edge closes it at once and goes to the Home screen.

## Settings

- **Time zone**: the app doesn't read the tablet's time zone. Tap the **UTC** button to add one hour (after +14 it wraps to -12). For example, Italy is UTC+1 in winter and UTC+2 in summer.
- Task, audio, goal, time zone and stats are saved on the SD card.
- The timer is saved too: when you exit, the block stops, and when you reopen the app you find it paused (PAUSED) where you left it. Tap **START** to carry on. Time spent with the app closed isn't counted. If you close it by swiping up instead of using **EXIT**, you may lose up to 10 seconds of the block.
- Stats reset by themselves when the day changes, going by the app's own clock (so by the **UTC** offset you set); a notice tells you when it happens. **CLEAR** erases them right away. If the tablet's clock hasn't been synced yet, the automatic reset waits.

## Common problems

- **The clock is off by an hour or two**: fix the time zone with the **UTC** button.
- **No sound**: make sure AUDIO isn't OFF, connect a speaker to the tablet and check the volume in Settings.
- **VOICE only plays tones**: the voice says a word at the end of a block, and only when the tablet is set to Italian. Otherwise you hear tones.
