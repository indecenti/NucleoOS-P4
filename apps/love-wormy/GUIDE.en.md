# Wormy

## What it is

The snake game: eat the apples, grow longer, do not bite yourself or the walls. Swipe to turn; keyboard: arrows.

## Controls

taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.
