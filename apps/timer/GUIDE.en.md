# Timer

## What it's for

A stopwatch and a countdown timer with big digits you can read from across the room. The countdown beeps when it reaches zero.

## How to use it

The app has two modes, shown at the top: **STOPWATCH** and **COUNTDOWN**.

Stopwatch:

1. Tap **START**: time starts from 00:00.
2. Tap **STOP** to pause it, then **START** to carry on from where you were.
3. Tap **RESET** to go back to 00:00.

Countdown:

1. With the timer stopped, tap **MODE** to switch to COUNTDOWN.
2. Set the minutes with **-1M** and **+1M** (1 to 99; it starts at 5:00).
3. Tap **START**. At 00:00 you hear a beep and the timer stops.
4. **RESET** puts the time back to the value you set.

## Controls

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| START / STOP | starts or stops the time |
| RESET | clears the stopwatch, or puts the countdown back to its start |
| MODE | switches between stopwatch and countdown (only while stopped) |
| -1M / +1M | removes or adds one minute to the countdown (only while stopped) |

To exit: swipe right from the left edge (Back gesture), or swipe up from the bottom edge to go to the Home screen.

## Tips

- Time is shown in minutes and seconds, up to 99:59.
- Changing mode resets the time.
- Settings aren't saved: when you reopen the app the countdown is back to 5 minutes.

## Common problems

- **I can't hear the final beep**: connect a speaker to the tablet and check the volume in Settings.
- **MODE, -1M or +1M don't respond**: stop the timer with **STOP** first.
