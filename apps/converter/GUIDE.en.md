# Converter

## What it is

Unit conversion for twelve quantities: length, mass, temperature, volume, area, speed, pressure, energy, data, time, angle and fuel consumption.

## How to use it

Pick a quantity on the left and type the value: every unit appears on the right at once. Tap a unit to type in that one. The last quantity and unit are remembered.

Keyboard: digits, comma or dot, Backspace, Esc to clear, Up and Down to change quantity.
