# Cannon

## What it's for

A small target range with a cannon. Your tank is on the left, a red target on the right: hit it with an arcing shot to score a point.

## How to use it

1. Put your finger on the screen and drag it: the barrel follows your finger and a line of white dots shows the path of the shot.
2. The farther your finger is from the cannon, the stronger the shot.
3. Lift your finger to fire.
4. If you hit the target there's an explosion, you score a point and the target moves somewhere else.

Your points are the small yellow squares at the top left.

## Controls

- **Drag your finger**: aim (direction and power together).
- **Lift your finger**: fire.
- While the shell is flying you can't aim: wait for it to land.
- To exit: swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen to go back to the Home screen. The Back gesture from the left edge does nothing in this game.

## Tips

- The dotted path is a guide: line it up with the target before you lift your finger.
- Points aren't saved: every time you open the game you start from zero.

## Common problems

- **I can't hear the shot**: connect a speaker to the tablet and check the volume in Settings.
