# Tank Rally

## What it is

A 3D arcade game with a little tank. In each arena you must pick up every coin before the clock runs out. Crates break open when shot and may hide coins or repair kits. Rocket launchers fire homing rockets at you: dodge them, shoot them down and knock the launchers out. There are five arenas (Meadow, Canyon, Sunset, Night, Snow), then it starts over with less time. Every run lays the arenas out anew.

## How to play

1. The title shows the five best scores. Tap the screen, or press A, to start.
2. The arena's name appears, then "READY... GO!".
3. Drive over the coins to pick them up. Coins taken one after the other in a short time build a combo and a bigger score. Each coin also adds 2 seconds.
4. Shoot the crates: each is worth 50 points and 3 seconds, and may hold a coin or a repair kit (+35 life).
5. When "ROCKET INCOMING!" shows, a red sight follows the rocket (an arrow when it is off screen). Rockets turn slowly: cut across their path and they miss, or shoot them down (+25). A launcher goes down with three hits (+150 points, +4 seconds).
6. With every coin taken the arena is clear: the time left turns into points and you get some life back.
7. The game ends when time runs out or your life reaches zero. If you make the table, enter your initials: arrows above and below the letters, then **OK**.

The game does not use the system gestures: the **II** button next to the clock pauses it (RESUME, MENU, QUIT THE GAME); the title has **QUIT**.

The life bar is top left, the clock in the middle, the map on the right (yellow coins, brown crates, red launchers).

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Keyboard | Gamepad | Mouse |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steer | ◀ ▶ bottom left | ← → or A D | left stick or d-pad | |
| Forward | GAS | ↑ or W | RT (or stick up) | right button |
| Reverse | BACK | ↓ or S | LT or B | |
| Fire | FIRE | Space or Ctrl | A, X or RB | left button |
| Turn the turret | (aims by itself) | Q / E | right stick | move the mouse |
| Pause | II button next to the clock | Start (P or Tab) | Start | |
| Quit | Pause > QUIT THE GAME, or QUIT on the title | Esc | Back | |

The turret aims by itself at the nearest crate or launcher in front of you. Turn it by hand and the auto-aim switches off for three seconds.

On touch you can slide your thumb from GAS to FIRE without losing speed. With a gamepad, keyboard or mouse the on-screen buttons disappear; touch the screen and they come back.
