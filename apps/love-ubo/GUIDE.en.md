# Ubo

## What it is

A Qix-like: draw lines to cut off parts of the field and claim them while the enemies roam. On-screen d-pad and Draw.

## Controls

On-screen buttons are drawn over the game; taps and swipes work as the mouse and arrow keys. A USB or Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad works too (pad: d-pad = arrows, A = Space, B = Ctrl, Start = Enter). The back gesture is Escape; twice in a row closes the game.
