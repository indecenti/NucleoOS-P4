# RetroLove

## What it is

Three arcade classics in one, vector style: **Pong**, **Breakout** and **Asteroids**. Tap the one you want in the menu.

## Controls

Touch: in Pong and Breakout keep a finger on the left or right side of the screen to move; in Asteroids the bottom corners rotate, thrust and fire. Tap the top centre to pause or resume.

Keyboard: arrows to move, Space to fire, Enter to pause and start, Esc back to the menu. The Back gesture works as Esc; twice in a row leaves the app.
